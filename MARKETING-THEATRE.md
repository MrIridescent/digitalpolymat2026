# The Iridescent Marketing Theatre

One file. Every stage. Every prompt.

A production manual for generating Mr Iridescent marketing assets with AI image,
video and copy models — portraits, campaign posters, social systems, merchandise,
motion, print, digital surfaces and the words that hold them together.

The work is art-directed, not generated by accident. Every scene below tells a
model what to make, what to leave empty, and what the typesetter will add later.
Nothing is crowded. Nothing is improvised.

**Brand:** Mr Iridescent — David Akpoviroro Oke
**Site:** mriridescent.africa
**Instagram:** @mriridescent
**Email:** me@mriridescent.africa

---

## 0. How to run a scene

1. **Choose the act and scene** for the asset you need (portrait, poster, social,
   merch, motion, print, digital, copy).
2. **Paste the Brand Constants** (section 1) into the model first, or keep them
   in a saved style reference.
3. **Generate at the listed ratio.** Do not crop a square into a story; generate
   for the final format.
4. **Typeset the words in post** — Figma, Photoshop or Affinity. Image models
   cannot set `mriridescent.africa` or `@mriridescent` reliably. Every prompt
   reserves clean space for that type. Section 12 gives the exact sizes.
5. **Run the quality gate** (section 13). If the frame feels full, remove
   something. Breathing room is a decision, not a happy accident.

> The model makes the atmosphere. The designer makes the brand.

## 2. The Breathing Room Law

Jampacked is the fastest way to look inexpensive. These rules are non-negotiable.

**The density veto.** If an element does not increase clarity or desire, delete
it. A poster is not a brochure. A feed post is not a pitch deck.

**Margins.** Keep at least 8% of the short edge empty on every side. Posters and
merch go to 12%. Nothing important lives in the outer 6%.

**One message per frame.** One headline, one support line, one call to action.
No bullet lists inside artwork. No three-column feature grids on a poster.

**Type limits.** Headline: six words or fewer. Support: eighteen words or fewer.
Labels: four words or fewer.

**Two typefaces maximum.** Instrument Serif + Inter, or Instrument Serif +
JetBrains Mono. Never three.

**Colour discipline.** Ink and ivory carry the frame. Acid appears once, as a
line, a number or a single word. Cyan and violet live in the light, not the type.

**Negative space targets.**

| Surface | Minimum empty space | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Instagram feed 4:5 | 35% | Headline in the upper third, URL bottom-left |
| Story / Reel 9:16 | 45% | Keep the middle 60% clear of UI overlays |
| Campaign poster | 45% | One image, one line, one URL |
| Merch front | 70% of garment | Small chest mark, nothing else |
| OG / link card | 40% | Text left, image right, no border clutter |
| Print cover | 50% | Paper is part of the design |

**The 12-column grid.** Even for a single image, imagine 12 columns and a
baseline grid. Align the headline to column 1, the URL to column 1, the handle to
column 12. Consistency is what makes a feed look designed rather than posted.

**Spacing scale.** 8 / 16 / 24 / 40 / 64 / 96 px at 1080 px width. Never invent
an in-between value. Space is a system, not a mood.

## 3. The master prompt formula

Every image prompt in this file follows the same order. You can write new scenes
without breaking the system.

```text
[ASSET + RATIO]
[SUBJECT] — who or what, specific and respectful
[STAGE] — place, surface, architecture, props (few)
[LIGHT] — key, rim, falloff, time of day
[COMPOSITION] — camera height, lens, crop, negative space reservation
[PALETTE] — ink, ivory, acid, cyan, violet, in proportion
[TYPE ZONE] — where the headline and furniture will be placed in post
[FINISH] — grain, contrast, texture, mood
[NEGATIVES] — what must not appear
[OUTPUT] — aspect ratio, resolution, format
```

**The universal suffix.** Append this to every still-image prompt:

```text
Editorial art direction, generous negative space, one focal point, matte
finish, fine 3–5% film grain, true-to-life skin and fabric texture, no text,
no lettering, no logos, no watermarks, no busy background, no HDR halo,
no oversaturation, no clip-art, no collage. Palette restricted to #0b0d12,
#f7f5ef, #c9f24b, #67e8f9 and #a78bfa.
```

**Likeness.** For portraits of David, use the supplied reference photograph with
a character-reference or image-to-image feature. State: "preserve facial
identity, skin tone and hairline exactly; change only wardrobe, light and set."
Never let a model invent a different face for the brand.

## 7. Act IV — The Merch Atelier

Merch is brand theatre you can wear. The rule is the same as the posters: one
mark, one line, one URL. If a garment needs three graphics to be interesting, it
is not designed yet.

### The merch system

**Three marks only.** Crowned MR monogram, "MR IRIDESCENT" wordmark, and the URL.
The Instagram handle appears once, small, on a sleeve, hem or interior label.

**Front is quiet. Back carries the message.** Chest mark 70–90 mm. Back line
240–280 mm wide. Nothing within 30 mm of a seam.

**One colourway per drop.**

| Drop | Name | Garment colour | Print | Accent |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 01 | Ink & Acid | Ink #0b0d12 | Ivory + acid | Acid |
| 02 | Paper & Ink | Ivory #f7f5ef | Ink + acid | Acid |
| 03 | Natural & Cyan | Natural canvas | Ink + cyan | Cyan |

**Print methods.**

| Method | Best for | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Screen print | Tees, totes, hoodies | Most durable, best for flat colour |
| DTF | Small runs, detailed art | Soft hand feel, good for gradients |
| Embroidery | Caps, hoodies, polos | Monogram only, 70 mm maximum |
| Foil | Notebooks, cards, packaging | Acid or ivory foil on ink |
| Woven label | Neck, hem, sleeve | URL on the reverse of the label |

**Artwork rule.** Generate the print graphic on a flat ivory or transparent field,
then composite it onto the garment in post. Do not ask an image model to draw the
URL on fabric. It will misspell it. Typeset the URL at the sizes in section 12.

### The shared wrappers

Use one artwork prompt plus one wrapper. This keeps the whole drop visually
consistent.

**WRAP-A — Studio flat lay**

```text
Top-down studio flat lay of [garment] in [colour], laid on warm ivory paper
(#f7f5ef) with a soft directional light from the upper left and a long gentle
shadow. The garment is neatly arranged, sleeves relaxed, no mannequin, no props,
no hangers. 70% of the frame is empty paper. 100mm, high detail, visible fabric
texture, no text, no logos other than the specified print.
```

**WRAP-B — On-body editorial**

```text
Editorial half-body photograph of a Black Nigerian man in his mid-thirties
wearing [garment], standing against a deep ink seamless backdrop, three-quarter
turn, calm expression, hands relaxed. Soft key from the left, subtle acid-lime
rim on the shoulder, 85mm at f/2.8. The garment print faces the camera clearly.
Generous negative space on the right for typeset copy. No text, no extra logos.
```

**WRAP-C — Macro detail**

```text
Extreme macro of the [placement] print on [garment], showing cotton weave, ink
absorption and a crisp edge. Raking side light, shallow depth of field, ink and
ivory palette with one acid-lime thread. 100mm macro, no face, no background
clutter.
```

## 11. Act VIII — The Copy Stage

Copy prompts are for a language model, not an image model. Feed it the brand
constants first, then one task.

### The voice

- Direct, specific, calm. Short sentences. No hype.
- One claim, one proof, one invitation.
- No exclamation marks, no emoji, no "unleash your potential", no "game-changer".
- Never overstate credentials. If a number is not verified, describe the work.
- Repeat the error once, not twice. When something fails, state it plainly.

### C-01 — Caption engine

```text
You are the copy director for Mr Iridescent (David Akpoviroro Oke), a Lagos-based
growth partner, PR and reputation strategist, and digital product delivery
architect. Voice: direct, warm, specific, no hype, no emoji, no exclamation marks.

Write one Instagram caption for [asset]. Structure:
1. Hook, maximum 12 words.
2. Two sentences of context that teach something true.
3. One line of proof or specificity.
4. One invitation, phrased as a question or a quiet instruction.
5. Furniture on separate lines: mriridescent.africa / @mriridescent

Maximum 110 words. Do not use hashtags in the body. Suggest five precise
hashtags at the end, no generic reach-bait.
```

Write a seven-page carousel for [topic]. Each page has a title of six words or

## 12. Post-production specification

Typography is applied by hand. These are the exact settings.

### Type scale

| Surface | Headline | Support | Label / URL | Handle |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| IG 4:5 (1080) | Instrument Serif 72–96 | Inter 34–38 | JetBrains Mono 30 | 26 |
| IG 1:1 (1080) | 64–84 | 32–36 | 28 | 24 |
| Story (1080) | 84–110 | 40–44 | 32 | 28 |
| LinkedIn 4:5 (1200) | 72–90 | 34–38 | 30 | 26 |
| OG (1200 × 630) | 56–64 | 24–26 | 22 | 20 |
| X (1600 × 900) | 64–76 | 28–30 | 26 | 22 |
| YouTube (1280 × 720) | 72–88 | 32 | 26 | 22 |
| A2 poster (4961 px) | 260–360 | 90–110 | 70 | 60 |
| Business card (300 dpi) | 24 pt | 8 pt | 6.5 pt | 6 pt |

**Tracking.** Instrument Serif: −1% to 0%. Inter: 0%. JetBrains Mono labels:
+14%, uppercase.

**Line height.** Display 1.02–1.08. Body 1.45–1.55. Labels 1.2.

**Alignment.** One left edge for all text on a surface. Never centre a headline
and left-align the URL in the same frame.

### Colour and export

- Design in sRGB. Convert to CMYK only for offset print, and check the acid
  (`#c9f24b`) — it shifts green in CMYK; ask for a proof.
- Export social as PNG for type, JPG at 90% for photography.
- Print: PDF/X-4, 300 dpi, 3–5 mm bleed, crop marks, fonts outlined.
- AI images for large print: generate at the highest native resolution, upscale
  once, then sharpen at 30–40% and add 3% grain to hide interpolation.
- Logo and type are always vector. Never upscale a raster logo.

### File naming

```text
mr-iridescent_[act]_[scene]_[format]_[campaign]_[version].[ext]

Examples:
mr-iridescent_portrait_i01_4x5_press_v1.png
mr-iridescent_campaign_ii01_4x5_growth_v2.png
mr-iridescent_merch_m02_flatlay_drop01_v1.png
mr-iridescent_social_carousel_cover_4x5_authority_v3.png
```

Keep a `_final` folder and an `_archive` folder. Never edit a final in place.

## 13. The quality gate

Run this before anything leaves the studio.

**Composition**

- [ ] One focal point, obvious within one second.
- [ ] At least 35% negative space on social, 45% on posters.
- [ ] Nothing important within 6% of the edge.
- [ ] The headline does not sit on a busy area of the image.
- [ ] The frame is readable at thumbnail size.

**Type**

- [ ] Maximum two typefaces.
- [ ] Headline six words or fewer.
- [ ] URL spelled exactly: `mriridescent.africa`.
- [ ] Handle spelled exactly: `@mriridescent`.
- [ ] Email spelled exactly: `me@mriridescent.africa`.
- [ ] No fake lettering anywhere in the generated image.
- [ ] Text has clear contrast; no ivory on ivory.

**Brand**

- [ ] Palette is ink, ivory, acid, cyan, violet — nothing else.
- [ ] The crowned MR mark is undistorted and has clear space.
- [ ] Acid is used once, not as a background wash.
- [ ] The image could not belong to another consultant.

**Craft**

- [ ] Hands, eyes and teeth survive a 200% zoom.
- [ ] Fabric and skin texture are real, not plastic.
- [ ] No HDR halo, no oversharpening, no rainbow gradient.
- [ ] Print files have bleed, crop marks and outlined fonts.
- [ ] The file name follows the convention.

**Message**

- [ ] One claim, one proof, one invitation.
- [ ] No unverified numbers or invented clients.
- [ ] The call to action is a URL, not a slogan.

If any box fails, fix it. If two boxes fail, start the scene again.
